Our Lifts

The right lift system for every building

From compact residences to high-rise towers and heavy industrial sites — we help you choose, install and maintain the system that fits.

Mid to high-rise

Traction Lifts

A motor drives a grooved sheave with steel ropes looped over it — the lift car on one end, a counterweight on the other. Simple, proven and highly energy-efficient.

  • Lower energy consumption
  • Smooth, fast rides for taller buildings
  • Long service life
Low to mid-rise

MRL Lifts (Machine Room Less)

The machine sits inside the hoistway, removing the need for a separate machine room. Ideal for low- to mid-rise buildings with up to 250 ft of rise.

  • Saves space and construction cost
  • Reliable, well-proven technology
  • Less routine maintenance
Heavy loads

Hydraulic Lifts

Pressurised fluid in a cylinder drives a piston upward. Powerful and robust — built for warehouses, factories, construction sites and other industrial settings.

  • High load capacity
  • Goods, car and industrial lifts
  • Scissor, two-post & four-post options
